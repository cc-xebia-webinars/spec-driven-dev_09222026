# Acme Corporation — Travel and Expense Policy

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Owner:** Finance Operations
**Applies to:** All employees and contractors submitting travel expenses

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes what Acme will reimburse when you travel for work, and what
documentation you need to provide. Finance reviews every submission against this
document. If something here is unclear, ask before you travel rather than after.

## 2. Meal per diem

Acme reimburses meals using a daily allowance rather than itemised receipts. The
allowance depends on the city tier of your destination.

| City tier | Examples | Daily meal allowance (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | New York, London, Tokyo, Zurich | 75 |
| Tier 2 | Berlin, Chicago, Madrid, Toronto | 60 |
| Tier 3 | All other destinations | 45 |

The allowance is a cap, not a payment. You claim what you actually spent, up to the
cap for that day.

Travel days where you are away for part of the day only are reimbursed at a reduced
rate. Finance applies the reduced rate to the first and last day of any trip.

## 3. Receipts

Receipts are required for individual expenses above a threshold. Below the threshold,
a line item with a description is enough. Finance may ask for a receipt on any item if
the submission looks unusual.

Expenses above the threshold that arrive without a receipt are returned to the
submitter. They are not paid pending follow-up.

## 4. Approval

Submissions route for approval based on their total value. Small submissions are
handled by the submitter's own manager. Larger submissions need a second approval
from a director. Very large submissions are reviewed by Finance directly before
any approval is sought.

The thresholds are reviewed annually and published on the Finance intranet page.

## 5. Foreign currency

Expenses incurred in a currency other than US dollars are converted to US dollars
before the policy caps are applied. Conversion uses the applicable rate.

Acme currently reimburses expenses in euros and pounds sterling. Other currencies
require prior arrangement with Finance.

## 6. What is not reimbursable

- Alcohol, except as part of a client entertainment expense with prior approval
- Personal entertainment, including in-room movies and gym fees
- Fines, penalties, and parking tickets
- Travel upgrades not authorised in advance

## 7. Submitting

Submit within 30 days of the last day of travel. Submissions older than 90 days
require a written exception from your director.
